#### Anti-Patterns Verdict
**LLM assessment**: The active test interface is structurally solid, but the Dashboard falls into the "strangeness without purpose" trap of AI-generated product UIs. The inclusion of a jQuery water ripple effect (jquery.ripples) on the welcome banner is the single biggest "AI hallucinated a cool feature" tell. It violates the Product register's strict ban on decorative motion that doesn't convey state. Furthermore, there are multiple drop shadows (shadow-2xl, shadow-md shadow-primary/20) that violate the anti-reference of "shadow-heavy UI".

**Deterministic scan**: The detector found 15 issues, primarily undocumented colors (various opacities of gba(14, 165, 233)) in index.css and #ccc in DesmosCalculator.tsx. It also flagged DM Sans being imported in index.css despite DESIGN.md specifying Inter. Lastly, it caught a contrast warning (gray text 	ext-slate-400 on a red background g-red-50) in ExamManagerPanel.tsx.

#### Overall Impression
The core test-taking experience is well-structured and functional (love the keyboard shortcuts!), but the surrounding UI suffers from "AI feature creep." Gratuitous motion (water ripples) and rogue styles (unplanned colors/fonts) make it feel less like a focused, premium product and more like a template. Stripping away the excess will instantly elevate it.

#### What's Working
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Implementing A/B/C/D and arrow keys for the active test is a fantastic power-user accelerator.
- **Split-Screen Layout**: The test interface honors the official Digital SAT layout, keeping passages and questions co-located nicely.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P0] Gratuitous Motion & Tech Debt**: The dashboard banner includes a legacy jquery.ripples effect. This is a massive violation of the product register (decorative motion), adds unnecessary weight, and looks wildly out of place for a serious SAT prep app.
  - **Why it matters**: It distracts users, slows down the page, and screams "I added this because I could, not because I should."
  - **Fix**: Remove the jQuery ripples effect completely. Keep the banner static and clean.
  - **Suggested command**: /impeccable distill

- **[P1] Design System Drift**: Undocumented colors and fonts (DM Sans instead of Inter, gba(14, 165, 233) blues instead of SAT Indigo) are leaking into the codebase.
  - **Why it matters**: Inconsistent typography and colors break the premium, trustworthy feel of an academic tool.
  - **Fix**: Clean up index.css, remove DM Sans, and map hardcoded values to DESIGN.md tokens.
  - **Suggested command**: /impeccable audit

- **[P2] Shadow-heavy & Glassmorphic UI**: shadow-2xl, shadow-primary/20, and ackdrop-blur-md are used in modals and headers.
  - **Why it matters**: This directly violates the project's anti-reference of a "2014-era app look; shadow-heavy or glassmorphism-heavy UI."
  - **Fix**: Flatten the layers. Use subtle borders or shadow-sm for structural depth only.
  - **Suggested command**: /impeccable quieter
